素月分辉，明河共影，表里俱澄澈。
悠然心会，妙处难与君说。

The pale moon spreads its glow, the Milky Way casts its reflection; above and below, all is clear.
My heart takes it in at ease, but the wonder of it is hard to put into words.

Zhang Xiaoxiang, Crossing Dongting Lake (to the tune Niannujiao)

Light I have chased around the world: city skylines at blue hour, fog rolling off the Pacific, and quiet corners of the places I have lived. Most of these were taken on a Nikon Z8. Click any photo to see it larger, and use the arrow keys to browse.
