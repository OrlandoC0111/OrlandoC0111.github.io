Current research

{% capture current %}### Soil environment and genotype effects on soybean rhizosphere bacteria *With Hajira Younas, Ryan Stewart, Bo Zhang and Huijie Gan, Virginia Tech* Plants recruit a subset of soil bacteria into the rhizosphere, but how much of that recruitment depends on the soil they grow in, and how much on the plant itself? To find out, we grew the same nine soybean lines, spanning high, medium and low root-to-shoot ratios, in replicated field plots at two Virginia sites with different soils: * **Blacksburg, VA**: Hayter loam (Ridge and Valley) * **Warsaw, VA**: Kempsville loam (Coastal Plain) We sequenced the 16S rRNA gene (V3–V4) of bulk soil and rhizosphere samples from every plot. All samples went through a single pipeline (primer trimming, DADA2, SILVA 138 taxonomy). We then asked: 1. How strongly does site (soil environment) structure bulk and rhizosphere communities? 2. Do soybean genotypes and root:shoot classes recruit different rhizosphere bacteria? 3. Which assembly processes (selection, dispersal limitation, drift) dominate at each site? {% endcapture %} {{ current | markdownify }}
