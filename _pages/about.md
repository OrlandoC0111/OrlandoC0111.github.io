I grew up in Jinan, Shandong, China, and I work where statistics, machine learning and biology meet, from soil microbes to drone imagery of crop fields.

**Environmental science (2019–2023).** I earned bachelor's degrees in environmental science from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) (Agricultural Resources and Environmental Science) and [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/) (Environment & Sustainability). At Cornell I wrote an honors thesis in soil microbiology with [Dr. Janice Thies](https://cals.cornell.edu/people/janice-e-thies). It looked at how biochar changes soil microbial communities and the soil exoenzymes they produce.

**Biostatistics (2023–2025).** I then completed a Master of Biostatistics (Mathematical Statistics track) at [Duke University School of Medicine](https://medschool.duke.edu/). There I worked on statistical genetics: my thesis compared methods that control confounding between guide RNA and gene expression in single-cell CRISPR screens. I also applied Bayesian latent class models and machine learning to electronic health records, to map the pain trajectories of breast cancer survivors.

**Now at Virginia Tech.** I am in the [School of Plant and Environmental Sciences](https://spes.vt.edu/), working with [Dr. Huijie Gan](https://spes.vt.edu/). <!-- TODO: replace with Dr. Gan's lab or faculty page URL; add your degree program (M.S./Ph.D.) if you want it here -->
My work combines soil microbiology, remote sensing and drone (UAV) applications in agricultural research, and machine learning for biological data. My current project asks how soil environment and plant genotype shape the bacterial communities around soybean roots, at two Virginia field sites with contrasting soils.

Research interests
------
* **Machine learning for biology and agriculture.** Prediction, classification and clustering for microbial, genomic, field and remote sensing data
* **Data science for complex biological data.** Longitudinal, high-dimensional and real-world data (EHRs, sequencing, sensor and imagery data)
* **Bayesian and latent variable modeling.** Finding hidden structure such as trajectories, subgroups and risk profiles in noisy data
* **Statistical genetics and genomics.** Inference and error control for high-throughput screens such as single-cell CRISPR experiments
* **Remote sensing and drones in agriculture.** Using UAV and satellite imagery to monitor crops and fields at scale
* **Soil and rhizosphere microbiology.** How soil environment and plant genotype shape microbial communities and their functions

Methods and tools
------
* **Statistical modeling:** generalized linear models, linear mixed-effects models, Bayesian statistics, Bayesian latent class models, latent class analysis, statistical inference and power analysis
* **Machine learning:** random forest, k-nearest neighbors, k-means clustering, predictive and risk-profile modeling
* **Statistical genetics:** single-cell CRISPR screen analysis (SCEPTRE, Biased Urn), type I error and power evaluation
* **Microbial ecology and bioinformatics:** 16S rRNA and ITS amplicon sequencing (QIIME 2, DADA2, cutadapt), ordination (PCoA) and PERMANOVA, differential abundance (ANCOM-BC2), community assembly analysis (iCAMP, βNTI), co-occurrence networks
* **Data and geospatial:** electronic health records and SQL (Duke PACE / Clinical Research Data Mart), ArcGIS, remote sensing and drone imagery
* **Programming and computing:** R, Python, SAS, SQL, Java; Linux, Slurm high-performance computing, Git, LaTeX; JMP, GraphPad Prism

Beyond the lab
------
Away from the screen and the field plots, you will most likely find me on my road bike. Blacksburg is a playground for it: empty farm roads that roll on forever, long climbs into the Blue Ridge, and descents fast enough to make every one of those climbs worth it. I love all of it: the cold early start, the steady grind up a mountain, and the moment the road tips downhill and the wind takes over.

A camera usually comes along, on rides and on trips, because the best light shows up when you least expect it. I keep a [map of everywhere I've been](/travel/), a [gallery of photos](/photography/) from the road, and [stories from my rides](/cycling/).
