I am a graduate student in the [School of Plant and Environmental Sciences](https://spes.vt.edu/) at Virginia Tech, working with [Dr. Huijie Gan](https://spes.vt.edu/). <!-- TODO: replace with Dr. Gan's lab or faculty page URL -->
I study the soil and root-associated (rhizosphere) microbiomes of crops: how soil environment and plant genotype shape these bacterial communities, and what processes, from selection to dispersal limitation, put them together.

My current project compares bulk soil and rhizosphere bacterial communities of nine soybean lines grown at two Virginia sites with contrasting soils: Blacksburg (Ridge and Valley) and Warsaw (Coastal Plain).

Research interests
------
* Rhizosphere microbial ecology and plant–soil–microbe interactions
* Microbial community assembly (deterministic vs. stochastic processes)
* Crop genotype effects on root-associated microbiomes
* Soil microbial processes linked to nitrogen cycling
* Reproducible amplicon sequencing (16S rRNA / ITS) bioinformatics

Methods and tools
------
16S rRNA and ITS amplicon sequencing (QIIME 2, DADA2, cutadapt), community ecology statistics in R (phyloseq, vegan, mixed models, PERMANOVA), differential abundance (ANCOM-BC2), community assembly analysis (iCAMP, βNTI), co-occurrence networks, and high-performance computing (Slurm).
