Du Fu, Officer Fang's Western Horse

Data and soil keep my mind busy. Sport keeps the rest of me honest. These days that mostly means a road bike, but a basketball court or a soccer pitch has never been far away, and on game day you will find me cheering for the Warriors (since 2014) and Barça (since 2009).

On two wheels

Road cycling is how I explore a place: slow enough to see it, fast enough to get somewhere. Around Blacksburg that means empty farm roads, long climbs into the Blue Ridge and descents that pay back every pedal stroke. My favorite rides are below, and every ride lands on Strava.

On the court and the pitch

Basketball

Pickup runs, the squeak of sneakers on hardwood, and the quiet satisfaction of a clean pass that turns into an easy bucket. Basketball taught me teamwork and how to read a game, and I still play whenever I can find a court and a few people ready to run.

Soccer

Ninety minutes of running, passing triangles and the rare perfect first touch. I love soccer for its patience: it rewards space, timing and trust in your teammates, much like good research.

In the stands

Golden State Warriors

Dub Nation since 2014

I have been with the Warriors since 2014, through the ball movement, the off-ball cutting and the threes from well beyond the arc that made basketball look like jazz. Salute to the core that built a dynasty: Stephen Curry, who changed how the game is played; Draymond Green, its heart and its defensive brain; and Klay Thompson, the other Splash Brother, who could catch fire at any moment.

FC Barcelona

Més que un club · since 2009

I have watched Barça since 2009: tiki-taka, La Masia and the blaugrana, playing soccer the way I love it, with possession, patience and moments of pure magic. And at the center of it all was Lionel Messi, the GOAT. Watching him week after week was a privilege. Visca el Barça!
