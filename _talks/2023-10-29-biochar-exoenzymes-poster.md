**Authors:** Orlando Chen, Janice E. Thies (Cornell University)

My undergraduate honors thesis. Using soils from South Carolina and Oregon, I tested how biochar amendment changes soil microbial communities and the activities of key soil exoenzymes. I sequenced the microbial communities and related biochar treatments to community structure and enzyme activity with PCoA, PERMANOVA and generalized linear models.
