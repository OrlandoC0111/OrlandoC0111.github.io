**Authors:** Orlando Chen, Hajira Younas, Ryan Stewart, Bo Zhang, Huijie Gan

We compared bulk soil and rhizosphere bacterial communities (16S rRNA, V3–V4) of nine soybean lines grown in replicated field plots at two Virginia sites with contrasting soils (Blacksburg and Warsaw). We asked how soil environment and plant genotype shape rhizosphere communities and which assembly processes dominate at each site.

Presented at CANVAS 2026 (ASA, CSSA, SSSA International Annual Meeting), Portland, OR, November 1–4, 2026.
